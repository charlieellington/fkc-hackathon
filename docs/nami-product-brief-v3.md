## What changed in v3 (GPT-5.5 Pro × the 4-lens synthesis)

Three decisions reverse or revive earlier calls — each is an upgrade, not a flip-flop:

1. **After Dark moves *before* the Question (was after).** End on the After Dark wink and the demo reads as a "sexy gimmick app"; end on the Question + a smart next action and it reads as a **relationship product**. The laugh comes mid-show; the *aww* and the *"that's clever"* close it. (GPT + the Claude·UX dissent I'd flagged both land here.)
2. **The anniversary "micro-plan" is revived (the 4 lenses had cut it).** The old version rang fake ("an offline app can't book a restaurant"). The new version **reuses Leo's just-revealed answer** — *"recreate Sunday coffee"* — and only sets a *local* reminder. It reads as intelligence because it connects dots the audience just watched appear. That fixes the exact objection that killed it.
3. **The landing page and the $72 traction joke are cut from the live run.** Open straight on Today (the hero), phone already unlocked. Say the joke verbally if you want it; don't spend 5 seconds of hero time on it. (The landing can still exist as a pitch asset — it's just not the demo's first tap.)

**Unchanged and confirmed by every source:** one device (never two phones), Pulse before Spark (one tap, inline on Today), the Question reveal is the emotional peak, fake-and-lock everything with no dead-clicks, end the *final frame* on the growing Flame.

## The one amazing thing

> **Nami makes the relationship feel alive: one tiny daily action warms the shared flame, unlocks your partner's hidden answer, and turns that moment into a specific next loving action.**

If judges remember one moment, it's Leo's blurred answer unlocking:
> *"Sunday morning — coffee, both of us, Theo still asleep. No phones. I felt like us again."*

That line **is** the product. Everything else is scaffolding that earns it and cashes it.

The four proof-points, ranked:
1. **The Question reveal** = the *aww* (emotional peak). Build it most beautifully, never sacrifice it.
2. **The Spark translation** = "it understands each of us, differently."
3. **The Love Score / Flame** = visual proof the relationship is responding, live.
4. **The anniversary micro-plan** = it turns feeling into action.

## The hero metric: the Love Score (frame it as a *signal*)

A single 0–100 number for "how are *we* doing," split into **Emotional** and **Intimate** sub-scores, embodied by the shared **Flame**. Demo state: **82, +4 this week · Emotional 88 · Intimate 71 · streak 47 · Level 6 Warm Hearth.**

**Language de-risk (load-bearing):** never call it a "grade" or "score out of ten for your marriage." Say **"signal," "early-warning light," "relationship pulse."** Keep the sub-scores visible so it feels *diagnostic, not judgmental*. The Intimate-71 gap + "5 weeks since a date" is the narrative engine — it's why everything downstream matters.

Target **80–85s, hard cap 90s.** If time collapses, cut from the bottom (After Dark first); **never sacrifice the Question.**

## Positioning & why it wins

Proactive not reactive (tend the fire before it's an emergency) · two-sided by design (the Spark's two nudges + the reciprocal Question prove it without a second device) · context no one else has (both Pulses + love languages + the calendar gap → a *specific* next action) · **a relationship product, not a gimmick** — encoded in the beat order itself · fun-as-a-moat (the Forecast wink, Ember's voice) · proven Duolingo habit mechanics pointed at love.

## Monetisation

Free = Pulse, Love Score, Spark, the Question, the Flame & streak. **Nami+ / After Dark (one sub per couple)** = the spicy/intimacy tier, the private Coach, deeper insights, the date-night planner. The After Dark wink in the demo is the paywall tease — desire and privacy are where willingness-to-pay lives.

## Roadmap (the cut list lives here — one slide, Q&A only)

Full quiz & archetype reveal · full Us & love-language profiles · calendar & cycle-aware mode · private Coach · tantra/intimacy depth · photo "Memories." Say "here's where it goes" — don't build or demo it.

Nami isn't the app you open when things are bad. It's the **30 seconds a day that means they never get that bad** — tiny daily reps for the relationship, so the spark never becomes an emergency. 🔥
